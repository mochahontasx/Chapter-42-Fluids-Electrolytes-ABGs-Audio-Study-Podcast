🔊 How to use
  • Pick a host below, then press the big ▶ play button — your device reads it aloud.
  • The silent video at the top stays pinned and animates to match what's being said.
  • The transcript highlights line-by-line; tap any line to jump there.
  • 🎙 Voice: natural UK, Australian/Irish & US English are listed first. You can also switch the audio to Spanish, French, or Russian.
  • Conversation shows (Break Room, The Nurses' Station) let you pick two different voices — one per speaker.
  • ⚡ Speed slider sets the pace. A floating ▶ button keeps the controls handy while you scroll.
